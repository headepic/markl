# Domain Research Report Templates

Use these structures when the user wants a concrete research deliverable. Adapt headings to the topic; do not mechanically fill sections that do not matter.

## Quick Scan

```markdown
# Quick Scan: {topic}

## TL;DR
- {one-sentence answer}
- {most important current fact}
- {most important caveat}

## What This Is
{plain-language definition and boundaries}

## Domain Map
| Area | What matters | Key actors / examples |
|---|---|---|

## Key Findings
1. **{finding}** — {evidence and confidence}
2. **{finding}** — {evidence and confidence}
3. **{finding}** — {evidence and confidence}

## What To Watch
- {trend, risk, pending decision, missing data}

## Sources
- [{source title}]({url}) — {why it matters}
```

## Deep Dive

```markdown
# Research Brief: {topic}

## Executive Summary
{5-10 bullets with the strongest conclusions}

## Scope And Assumptions
- Research object:
- User intent:
- Time/geography scope:
- Out of scope:

## Domain Map
| Component | Role | Main actors | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|

## Core Concepts
| Term | Working definition | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|

## Vertical Axis: How We Got Here
{narrative history, inflection points, path dependencies}

## Horizontal Axis: Current Landscape
{current players, approaches, substitutes, competitive map}

## Evidence Matrix
| Claim | Sources | Evidence type | Confidence | Recency risk | Caveat |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Counterarguments
| Claim challenged | Counterevidence | My read |
|---|---|---|

## Synthesis
{what the vertical and horizontal axes reveal together}

## Future Scenarios
| Scenario | Why it could happen | Signals to watch |
|---|---|---|

## Recommendations
{actions, decisions, reading, experiments, or monitoring plan}

## Open Questions
- {unknown or unresolved question}

## Sources
- [{source title}]({url}) — {access date if useful}
```

## Competitive Map

```markdown
# Competitive Map: {category}

## Category Definition
{what counts and what does not}

## Segments
| Segment | User job | Representative players |
|---|---|---|

## Players
| Player | Positioning | Strengths | Weaknesses | Pricing / business model | Best fit |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Decision Guide
| If you care about... | Prefer | Why |
|---|---|---|

## Structural Takeaway
{why the category is shaped this way}
```

## Reading Path

```markdown
# Reading Path: {topic}

## Goal
{what the learner should understand or be able to do}

## Prerequisites
- {concept}

## Stage 1: Orientation
- {source} — {why first}

## Stage 2: Core Understanding
- {source} — {what to extract}

## Stage 3: Frontier / Debate
- {source} — {what controversy or latest work it represents}

## Practice / Exploration
- {small project, exercise, or comparison}

## Glossary
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
```
